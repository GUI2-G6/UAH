UAH Privacy Policy

Unified Application Hub (UAH) is a student-built, open-source project. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what controls you have.

What we collect

  • Account data such as email, username, and password hash.
  • Resume uploads and extracted parsing data.
  • Application tracking data including status, notes, and dates.
  • Optional Gmail integration metadata when you explicitly opt in.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell user data.
  • We do not share user data for advertising or broker use.
  • We do not use personal data for ad targeting.

Your controls

  • Disconnect Gmail integration at any time.
  • Delete uploaded resumes.
  • Delete your account.
  • Contact us for privacy requests and corrections.

For the full repository policy text, see PRIVACY.md.

Privacy contact: privacy@uahapp.com
