Unified Application Hub — free, open-source job search and application tracking

UAH brings job discovery, resume parsing, and application tracking into one workspace. It is free to use, ad-free, and open source. No login or account is required to view this home page or the public policy pages linked below.

UAH unifies the fragmented job search experience: pulling listings from multiple providers into a single normalized index, parsing your resume, and tracking the status of every application in one place — without selling user data, profiling for ads, or sharing information with employers.
